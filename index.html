About

Between a camera
and a code editor.

I'm Aiden Jinseo Choi — I work between photography and software, and between Seoul and Vancouver.

Photography came first: a way to pay attention and keep the things that would otherwise pass. The rest grew from the same habit — I build iOS apps like ThinkTwin, handcraft keycaps under AC Studios, and run a food-and-drink channel, Booze & Foods, on YouTube.

Right now I'm doing my service in the Korean military, still making things in the margins. This is where the work lives.
